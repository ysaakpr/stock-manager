## What was run

Pre-registered in `ops/studies/preregistration-m14-9-momentum-turn-entry-2026-11-05.md` before any run. D13 (`PAPER_RATIFIED_2026_09_06`) reads the regime — NIFTY 50 TRI against its 200-session average — only on the monthly rebalance session. Each variant is D13 with one switch (`early_entry`) that may re-enter a **parked** book between rebalances; exits stay D13's monthly rule. Arm S (pre-registration §8) changes only the order buys reach A8's sector rail, and is listed when its runs are rendered:

* **Momentum v2, D13 paper config** — the paper book's configuration (D13): M9.5's toggles without the 15% vol target.
* **V1 confirmed cross (5 sessions >= MA200)** — a parked book re-enters after the NIFTY 50 TRI has closed at or above its 200d MA on 5 consecutive sessions; exits monthly, as D13.
* **V2 momentum turn (MA50 rising, 20d return > 0, >= 0.98 MA200)** — a parked book re-enters when the TRI is >= 0.98 x its 200d MA, its 50d MA is above its value 10 sessions earlier and its 20-session return is positive; exits monthly.
* **V3 scale-in (half at first close >= MA200)** — the first close at or above the 200d MA deploys half the basket; the next monthly rebalance completes it if risk-on, else parks again.
* **V4 breadth turn (>= 50% above own MA200, rising over 20d)** — a parked book re-enters when >= 50% of the PIT universe closes above its own 200d MA and that share is higher than 20 sessions earlier; exits monthly.

Mandate (owner, 2026-09-07): rank on XIRR ÷ max drawdown; both liquidity floors (₹1 cr and ₹10 cr median daily turnover); the decade, the six-year window and the walk-forward (choose on 2016-09..2021-08, verify on 2021-09..2026-08), never averaged; bar: backtested XIRR > 25%. Book corporate actions and interest on idle cash on; every order through A8's ratified rails.

## Analysis and recommendation (hand-written, 2026-11-06)

*Written by hand from the generated tables above, which it does not change. Cell counts come from
the generated scorecard and decision-rule tables; other figures are copied from the tables. This
section covers V1–V4. Arm S (pre-registration §8) is reported separately once its re-run
finishes; see "Status of arm S" below.*

### What the pre-registered rule concludes

**No variant is recommended. D13 stays as ratified, and no parallel paper book follows from this
study.**

1. **The walk-forward chose V2, the momentum turn.** On the floor-only selection window at
   ₹1 cr, V2's XIRR/DD was 0.854 (24.97% / 29.24%) against D13's 0.847 (24.29% / 28.69%). Both
   round to 0.85 in the table. The lead is under 1%, and it came with a deeper drawdown
   (+0.55pp). At ₹10 cr V2 led more clearly: 0.70 against 0.66.
2. **V2 then failed all three of the rule's conditions** (decision-rule table):
   - *Verification XIRR/DD at least D13's at both floors:* **no.** At ₹1 cr it collapsed: 1.45
     against D13's 1.93 (28.69% / 19.77% against 33.04% / 17.10%, so −4.35pp XIRR and +2.67pp
     drawdown). At ₹10 cr it edged D13, 1.23 against 1.20.
   - *XIRR/DD at least D13's in 6 of the 8 floor-only cells:* **no**, only 3 of 8. It loses the
     decade and the six-year window at both floors.
   - *Max drawdown within 2pp of D13's in every floor-only cell:* **no.** The worst is +2.67pp,
     in the ₹1 cr verification cell.
3. **None of the other variants passes either.** V1 holds 1 of 8 cells and V3 none; V4 holds 1
   of 8 but is the only variant inside the 2pp drawdown limit. The rule applies its conditions to
   the chosen arm only, so this is for the record.

The selection window picked the variant that then lost the decisive verification cell by a wide
margin. That is what a one-split walk-forward exists to catch: a sub-1% edge on five years of
selection data, chosen from five arms on a lake that has already seen many configurations, is
mostly noise.

### NIFTY 500, the paper book's own universe (attribution only)

The paper book trades the NIFTY 500 screen at the ₹1 cr floor (`UniverseParameters()`). These
rows are attribution only: the PIT membership opens 2016-10-24, so there is no NIFTY 500

  - Six-year ₹1 cr: 1.26 against 1.24.
  - Six-year ₹10 cr: 1.29 against 1.23.
  - Verification ₹1 cr: 1.18 against 1.02, with a shallower drawdown (19.76% against 22.26%).
  - Verification ₹10 cr: loses, 0.87 against 0.93.
* **V1 looks better on NIFTY 500 than anywhere else.** It beats D13 in three of the four cells
  and is first in two of them: six-year ₹1 cr (1.35 against 1.24), and verification ₹10 cr (1.26
  against 0.93). But it loses 7 of the 8 floor-only cells,
  including the selection window that would have to choose it.
* **These wins do not change the conclusion.** Adopting V2 or V1 on the NIFTY 500 rows would be
  choosing on test data, the reason M14.5 did not adopt its 2% band.
* **They do suggest what a follow-up could test.** Early entry looks more favourable on the
  narrower large/mid-cap screen than on the floor-only one. A NIFTY 500 walk-forward becomes
  possible once the pre-2016-10-24 membership build (M14.1) is published to the lake.

### Why early entry did not help in this backtest

* **Re-entries are few.** Over the floor-only decade, each variant re-entered early only 5–11
  times. Every one is confirmed by the fill ledger, so the replayed rule and the run agree. The
  differences are a handful of episodes, not a steady edge.
* **The re-entries that reversed within a month cost more than the good ones earned.**
  - **V3** (half the basket at the first close at or above the MA) is M14.5's daily re-entry at
    half size. Of its 11 decade re-entries, 3 were parked again at the next rebalance and 7 saw
    the TRI back below MA200 within 21 sessions. It is worse than D13 in 11 of 12 cells, with
    the deepest drawdowns of the study (up to +8.32pp).
  - **V2** fires below the MA by design, so its "below MA200 within 21 sessions" count (3 of 6)
    partly measures that. None of its 6 decade re-entries was parked again at the next
    rebalance. Its loss shows as the deeper drawdown in the verification cell (19.77% against
    17.10% at ₹1 cr), not as frequent whipsaw.
  - **V1**'s five-session confirmation did not remove whipsaw: 1 of 7 re-entries was re-parked
    and 3 of 7 reversed below the MA. It added the deepest decade drawdown after V3: 34.93%
    against 28.69%.
* **Costs are modest and not the reason.** Charges run 0–21% above D13's (V2: +1% to +15%; for
  example ₹2.42L against ₹2.20L on the ₹1 cr decade). One-way turnover rises from
  2.0–2.65x a year to at most 3.06x. The difference is the timing, not the friction.

### The A8 8-name floor (the M14.5 caveat), and why it matters more for the live book

* **In every backtest row, a parked book is not in cash.** A8's minimum-holdings rail refuses any
  sell that would take the book below 8 names. The refusal counts are nearly identical across
  arms (234–241 on the ₹10 cr decade) because every arm parks on the same risk-off rebalances.
* **So an early re-entry in these runs moves the book from about 8 names plus cash to 20 names,
  not from cash to invested.** That damps both the cost of waiting for the month and the gain or
  loss from re-entering early. The measured deltas are smaller than they would be for a book that
  parks fully.
* **The live paper book is the fully parked case.** It has held ₹10,00,000 in cash and no
  positions since its first session on 7 Oct 2026, through the 5 Nov session (read-only query of
  `paper_session.expected_book`). Its first session was a risk-off rebalance with nothing to
  sell, so the floor never kept a name. For this book an early re-entry is the whole basket at
  once. A month of waiting is worth whatever the market does in that month — larger, in both
  directions, than the backtest's partly invested parks suggest.
* **This does not overturn the rule.** It means the backtest understates the stakes of the timing
  choice for this book. It does not say which way they cut.
