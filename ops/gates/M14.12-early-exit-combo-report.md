## What was run

Pre-registered in `ops/studies/preregistration-m14-12-early-exit-combo-2026-11-07.md` before any code or run. The universe is the PIT NIFTY 500 screen; windows, floors and the mandate are M14.10's. The arms:

* **Momentum v2, D13 paper config** — the paper book's configuration (D13): M9.5's toggles without the 15% vol target.
* **C0 V2 + S+refill (paper candidate)** — PAPER_CANDIDATE_V2_SREFILL_2026_11_07 exactly: V2's momentum-turn re-entry with S+refill's rank-ordered, sector-refilled buys; exits monthly, as D13.
* **C1 C0 + daily exit, 2% band** — as C0, and an invested book is parked on the first non-rebalance session the TRI closes below 0.98 x its 200d MA; it re-enters only by C0's rules.
* **C2 C0 + daily exit, unbanded** — as C0, and an invested book is parked on the first non-rebalance session the TRI closes below its 200d MA; it re-enters only by C0's rules.

**Spell rule (pre-registration §3.2).** A park (a risk-off monthly rebalance, or in C1/C2 a daily exit) opens a parked spell; a re-entry (a risk-on monthly rebalance, or V2 firing on a non-rebalance session) ends it, so V2 re-enters at most once per spell and each park opens a new one. On a non-rebalance session the daily exit is read before V2, so a session is at most one park or one re-entry. A parked book is never re-parked daily.

Mandate (owner, 2026-09-07): rank on XIRR ÷ max drawdown; both liquidity floors (₹1 cr and ₹10 cr median daily turnover); the decade, the six-year window and the walk-forward (choose on 2016-09..2021-08, verify on 2021-09..2026-08), never averaged; bar: backtested XIRR > 25%. Book corporate actions and interest on idle cash on; every order through A8's ratified rails.

## Analysis and recommendation (hand-written, 2026-11-08)

*Written by hand from the generated tables above, which it does not change. Ratios to three
places are computed from the same run summaries (`results.json`); the tables show two.*

### What the pre-registered rule concludes

**No exit arm is recommended. D13 stays as ratified, and the M14.11 candidate book (C0) is
unchanged.**

1. **The walk-forward chose C2**, the unbanded daily exit. On the NIFTY 500 selection window at
   ₹1 cr, XIRR/DD was:
   - C2: 1.269 (24.74% / 19.49%)
   - C1: 1.071
   - C0: 1.051
   - D13: 0.899
2. **C2 fails all three conditions, against both comparators** (generated verdict above):
   - **Verification at both floors: no.**
     - ₹1 cr: 0.850, against D13's 1.025 and C0's 1.076.
     - ₹10 cr: 0.912, against D13's 0.926 and C0's 0.955.
   - **At least 6 of 8 cells: no.** It is at or above D13 in 4 of 8 cells and at or above C0 in
     2 of 8.
   - **Drawdown within 2 pp in every cell: no.**
     - Six-year ₹1 cr: 25.35%, +3.79 pp against D13 and +3.71 pp against C0.
     - Six-year ₹10 cr: +3.40 pp against D13.

   It loses both six-year cells by a wide margin: 1.016 and 1.073, against D13's 1.236 and 1.233.
3. **C2 fails by every measure, not by a hair.** It leads only on the window that chose it, and
   only at ₹1 cr. At ₹10 cr on that same window it is level with C1 (0.882 against 0.879).

### Why the daily exit fits the selection window and fails out of sample

* **C2's selection lead is a shallower drawdown, not a higher return.** Its selection-window
  drawdown is 19.49%, against 24.74% for C0 and 24.67% for D13, while its XIRR is lower than
  C0's (24.74% against 26.01%). The window's deepest fall is most likely the 2020 crash, which a
  daily exit leaves earlier; this was not traced session by session.
* **It paid for that in whipsaw everywhere else.** This is the interaction the pre-registration
  predicted (§3.2): V2 re-enters from 0.98 × MA200, and C2 exits anywhere below MA200.
  - Over the decade at ₹1 cr, C2 parked 42 times (40 of them daily exits), against 9 for D13
    and C0.
  - 32 of its 41 re-entries were followed by another park within 21 sessions.
  - Round trips are 970 against D13's 565 (1.7×). One-way turnover is 3.65× a year against
    2.27×.
  - Charges are ₹2.91L against ₹1.71L (+70%), which is +41% over C0.
  - In the verification window, charges are up 62% to 73% over C0.
* **Out of sample, the exits did not protect.** In the six-year and verification windows C2's
  drawdown is *deeper* than D13's and C0's, not shallower. The likely mechanism, consistent with
  the reversal counts but not traced session by session: its daily parks sold short dips below
  MA200 that recovered, it bought back higher, and the round trips added cost.
* **This repeats M14.5.** There, the unbanded daily exit (with daily re-entry) lost in 11 of 12
  cells. Pairing it with a V2 re-entry and the sector refill does not change its character.

### C1, the 2% band: better behaved, still not recommendable

* **The band removes most of the whipsaw.**
  - Over the decade, C1 made 14 parks (10 daily) and 6 of its 14 re-entries were reversed
    within 21 sessions.
  - Charges are 10% to 19% above C0's.
* **It beats C0 in 6 of 8 cells and shallows the drawdown in 6 of 8.** It is the best arm in the
  decade and six-year cells, for example 1.357 against C0's 1.328 at six-year ₹1 cr.
* **But it loses verification to C0 at both floors**: 1.063 against 1.076 at ₹1 cr, and 0.841
  against 0.955 at ₹10 cr. At ₹10 cr it also loses to D13 (0.926), with a drawdown 1.70 pp
  deeper.
* **The walk-forward did not choose it, so the rule cannot recommend it.** Even ignoring the
  rule, it fails the verification condition against C0.

### Reproducibility check: D13 and C0

* **All 16 D13 and C0 runs reproduce M14.10's exactly.** Their replay digests (journal, book and
  rails, byte for byte) equal those of M14.10's runs of the same strategy and window. For
  example, C0's ₹1 cr verification row is 23.26% / 21.61% / 1.076 in both studies.
* **The spec digests differ, and the cause is identified.** Since M14.10's campaign, the lake's
  corporate-action calendar grew (36,628 to 36,673 actions) and the split-rescale fingerprint
  moved. Both are fingerprinted in the spec, and neither changed a decision on these windows.
* **The M14.12 composition change left both arms untouched,** as the unit tests pin on the
  scripted replay.

### Ledger confirmation

* **Every park is confirmed by a sale on the following session.**
* **A few re-entries are not confirmed by a purchase**, all in the exit arms: C2 has 2 to 4
  unconfirmed per cell, and C1 has 1 or 2 in some cells.
* **Likely cause, not checked one by one:** these re-entries come one or two sessions after a
  daily park, while the park's sale proceeds are still settling (T+1). With no free cash, there
  is no purchase that next session.
* D13 and C0 are fully confirmed.

### Against the >25% XIRR bar

* **10 of 32 runs clear it:** D13 2 of 8, C0 3 of 8, C1 3 of 8, C2 2 of 8. All are six-year
  cells, plus the ₹1 cr selection window for C0 and C1.
* **No arm clears it in any decade cell.** The best decade XIRR is 24.20% (C0, ₹1 cr).
* No verification cell clears it either.

### Trial count and forking paths

* **This lake has now seen more than 70 configurations.**
* **C0's and D13's rows on every window had been read before this study** (M14.10), and C0 was
  picked for paper after its verification rows were seen.
* **The daily exit's poor record was known from M14.5.**
* The only out-of-sample element was the selection window's choice among four arms. That choice
  failed verification, so the honest reading is this:
  - an early exit adds nothing robust to V2 + S+refill on NIFTY 500;
  - a 2% band makes it less harmful, not useful.

### Recommendation

1. **Keep D13 as ratified, and keep the M14.11 candidate book (C0) as it is.** Neither exit arm
   earned a place beside it.
2. **Do not pursue a daily regime exit further on this lake.** Two pre-registered studies (M14.5,
   M14.12) agree that it fits crash periods and loses elsewhere. A banded variant tuned beyond 2%
   would be searching for a parameter on a lake that has been read too often.
3. **The forward paper book remains the test that matters for C0.**
