# M15.3 — The paper session on the live path's staging, reconciliation and kill switch

**Date:** 2026-10-08 (IST) · **Branch:** `polly/m15-3-paper-shared-path` · **Readiness item:** (c),
invariant #5 ("one decision path for paper and real") ·
**Status: code, migration, tests and runbook complete. Nothing changes live until 0015 is applied
and the scheduler restarted (§5). The paper book is all cash and October's rebalance is done, so
the first session that can trade through the new path is November's rebalance.**

## 1. The gap

The daily paper session (M13.1, `backtest/paper_session.py`) ran `ReplayEngine` → A8 →
`SimBroker` and placed straight onto the broker. The three `execution` pieces a real-money loop
runs were never on that path: `StagingCoordinator` (kill switch before every placement, then the
order journal), `Reconciler` (internal book against the broker after the fills), and `KillSwitch`.
"Paper and real share one decision path" held for the decision and not for its execution.

## 2. What changed

| Where | What |
|---|---|
| `backtest/paper_session.py` `_PaperBroker` | `place` goes through `StagingCoordinator.stage` (kill switch → `SimBroker.place` → `InMemoryOrderJournal`, deterministic uid `<book>:<decided>:<seq>`). `execute_session` books the session's corporate actions, then `StagingCoordinator.execute` fills the staged orders into the paper book's accounting book, then `Reconciler.reconcile` runs before the policy is asked. Orders still staged on a restored broker are re-entered into the order journal from `SimBrokerState.staged`. |
| `run_paper_session` | Takes a required `kill_switch` (and an optional recon `alerter`). Tripped: one `SKIPPED_DATA_RED` with `payload.event = KILL_SWITCH_TRIPPED`, verdict `HALTED`, nothing staged. Every decided session ends with one `HEARTBEAT` (`payload.event = RECONCILIATION`, with evidence of both sides, and the broker ids staged and filled). A break means the switch trips (source `RECON`), staging refuses, the session is recorded `RECON_BREAK` and journaled `ESCALATE` (`payload.event = RECON_BREAK`, key `RECON:<date>`, terms = digest of the breaks), and later sessions are refused until the switch is reset and a `paper_session_resolution` row names the break. This is the corporate-action escalation pattern, with the same table and the same `_skip`. A session that would decide past an unresolved break, or a skipped recon, raises instead. |
| Accounting book | A `PortfolioBook` persisted per session (`expected_book`) and restored from it, so the two sides of recon are built independently across days. It is seeded from the broker in only two cases: a pre-0015 row (`recon.seeded`), and the session after a *resolved* break, where the resolution is the owner's acceptance of the broker's side. Late dividends and late splits are now posted to both books (`PortfolioBook.credit_late_dividend`, new). |
| `execution/staging.py`, `execution/recon.py` | `StagingCoordinator.book` and `Reconciler.book` are widened to protocols (`FillLedger`, `ReconBook`) that `InternalBook` still satisfies. There is no behaviour change for existing callers. |
| `execution/kill_switch.py` | `kill_switch_path(data_root, account)` → `<DATA_ROOT>/kill_switch/<account>.json`, and an operator CLI `python -m execution.kill_switch {status,trip,reset}` (`status` exits 3 when tripped). |
| `dataplatform/store/migrations/0015_paper_session_recon.sql` | Adds `recon`, `expected_book` and outcome `RECON_BREAK`, and rewrites the 0012 constraints so they say the same about `COMPLETED`/`SKIPPED_DATA_RED`. A `RECON_BREAK` row stages nothing and carries a BREAK recon. A `COMPLETED` row's recon is clean or NULL. |
| `GET /status/paper` | Per book: kill-switch state (an unreadable file is never reported armed), latest outcome and recon, and unresolved escalations and breaks. A switch-refused session is a *successful* job run, so `/status/jobs` alone cannot show it. |
| `ops/runbooks/daily-eod.md` | Step 7, where the switch lives, trip/status/reset, how to read and resolve a break, `/status/paper`, and migrate-before-restart. |

`execution.kite_broker` is still unreachable from the paper path. The shared modules do not import it (an AST test checks this). The end-to-end scheduler probe now also asserts that staging, recon and the kill switch *were* loaded and that no kite module was.

## 3. Evidence

### Trading outcome unchanged (`tests/unit/test_paper_session_shared_path.py`)

`test_four_months_through_the_shared_path_trade_exactly_as_the_plain_replay` runs 2026-09-28 →
2026-12-31 (68 sessions). The run includes the opening basket (9 buys), the October and November
rebalances that turn it over (2 buys + 1 sell, then 1 buy + 2 sells), a risk-off rebalance on
2026-12-01 that sells (2 sells, down to the rails' eight-name holdings floor), and an on-time
dividend and a 2:1 split on held names. It is
decided one process per day through the new path. The baseline is one plain `ReplayEngine` walk
over the same world: `SimBroker` under `_AccountingBroker`, with no staging, recon or switch. The
pre-M15.3 paper session was already pinned equal to that walk. The test asserts:

- **Trades:** the concatenated fills are byte-identical (canonical JSON, every Decimal by its
  string), BUY and SELL both present.
- **Costs:** the summed `fill.cost.total` equals the walk's `total_charges`, as `Decimal`.
- **Book:** the last persisted `SimBrokerState` document equals the walk's, and the per-session
  ledgers concatenate line for line to the walk's ledger.
- **Cash:** broker cash equals the walk's (`Decimal`), and the persisted accounting book's cash
  equals the walk's `PortfolioBook` cash and the broker's `cash_value`.
- **Journal:** each decided session has exactly one added entry, its last: a clean
  `RECONCILIATION` `HEARTBEAT` whose staged/executed ids match the session's orders and fills.
  With those removed, the journal equals the walk's entry for entry.

**Result: equal.** The test takes 1.6 s.

### Postgres (`tests/integration/test_paper_session_job.py`, `test_status_api.py`)

- **Break through the real job:** stored `RECON_BREAK` (round-trips, summary carries the break,
  restore point is the break's book) → switch file under `data_root` tripped (source `RECON`) →
  `HALTED` → reset alone still refused, naming the break → resolution row → decided, `seeded`,
  clean.
- **Backward compatibility:** a scratch DB is migrated to 0014. A row is written with the M13.1
  writer's columns, then 0015 is applied (exactly `["0015"]`). The row reads back with
  `recon`/`expected_book` NULL and the same digest, and the next session decides with
  `recon.seeded` and clean.
- **Constraints:** the table refuses a `RECON_BREAK` that claims orders.
- **`/status/paper`:** empty; break + tripped switch → unhealthy with `RECON:<date>@<terms>`
  unresolved → reset + resolution → unresolved empty; an unreadable switch file is unhealthy,

## 4. What it does not do

- **The order journal is in memory, per run.** The durable record of what was staged is the
  `paper_session` row: the orders, the broker's staged orders in `book_state`, and the ids in
  `recon`. `PostgresOrderJournal` (`order_`) needs a `case_` row per order, and the paper book is
  not a case (D13 ratified a sleeve configuration). Moving paper orders into `order_` is a
  follow-up, not this task.
- **Red-day semantics are unchanged (D24).** On a halted day, as on a red one, orders staged for
  that session lapse unfilled. A real broker would fill them, because the switch halts *placement*.
  This is the existing paper rule, kept as it was.
- **The alerter is the logging floor** (`recon.alert` at error level). Routing it to the M13.2
  failure-alert channel is a follow-up.

## 5. After merge

1. `make migrate` **before** restarting the scheduler. The new code writes 0015's columns, so a
   scheduler running it without 0015 fails every paper run until the migration is applied.
2. `XDG_RUNTIME_DIR=/run/user/$(id -u) systemctl --user restart scheduler`.
3. After the next run: `GET /status/paper` is healthy. The first post-merge `paper_session` row
   has `recon->>'status' = 'CLEAN'` and `recon->>'seeded' = 'true'`, because the live rows predate
   0015, so the accounting book is seeded once from the broker. Every row after that should be
   `seeded = false`.
