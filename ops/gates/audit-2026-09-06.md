**A data fact that governs how every fundamentals number below must be read:** the PIT store's
newest filing is 2025-02-14 for the 90th-percentile ISIN (filings per year — 2024: 13,168; 2025:
2,909, all before mid-February; 2026: 10). As of 2026-01-01 zero of 1,598 ISINs have a filing
under 200 days old. The fundamentals arms therefore hold no rankable names after about September
2025 and sit in cash, while momentum keeps trading. The same staleness applies to the analyst's
live fundamentals evidence (T1/T2, BC1).

**Root cause, found after the owner's go (§7):** the feed changed, not the campaign. NSE's
`corporates-financial-results` stopped receiving new periods after the quarter ended December
2024 — probed live it returns 3,865 entries for Jan-Mar 2025 and then 28, 8, 59, 15, 7 per
quarter, all defunct companies filing old quarters. From the March-2025 quarter results are
published only through SEBI's Integrated Filing regime (`api/integrated-filing-results`, paged,
26,610 records on 2026-09-06, no ISIN, no period start, a new `in-capmkt` taxonomy). The new
feed is built (`--feed integrated`, commit `f075838`, verified on four captured documents and one
captured page) and its campaign is the forward sync.

## 5. Portfolio-management improvements shipped as prototypes (a-priori, toggled, off by default)

1. **Redeploy sale proceeds the next session** (`MomentumV2Parameters.redeploy_next_session`).
   A rebalance's sells fill T+1 and the cash then waited a month; with banding that is 10-25% of
   the book idle every month. The toggle deploys the freed cash into the basket already chosen,
   at the next session's prices, through the same allocator.
2. **Momentum + value blend** (`FundamentalsSignal.MOMENTUM_VALUE`): rank-average of trailing
   earnings yield and 12-1 momentum over names with positive trailing earnings — the two premia
   with the most negative long-run correlation, now measurable because the PIT fundamentals exist.
3. **Fundamentals policy family** (VALUE / GROWTH / QUALITY_VALUE) with the same mechanics as
   momentum v2, so any difference in the report is the signal.

Not done, deliberately (needs the owner): re-deriving the PIT store from L0 to purge the 65 DTIL
filings; quantising SimBroker fills to the exchange's actual tick regime (₹0.05 above ₹250 since
2024) rather than the paisa; a portfolio-level volatility target and drawdown de-risking overlay
(sketched, not built).
