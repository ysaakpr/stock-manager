## 1. Pre-ISIN resolution (`dataplatform/identity/pre_isin.py`)

**The problem.** The NSE bhavcopy published no ISIN column until 2011-06-22, so W1 enumerated
1,652,571 E1 rows into quarantine. A current-day listing cannot fill the gap. It is
survivorship-biased, symbols get recycled, renames move a company to a new symbol, and a
face-value change re-issues the ISIN while the symbol stays the same.

**The resolver walks the exchange's own `PREVCLOSE` chain backwards.** It starts from each
symbol's first row in the ISIN era, which states the ISIN, and admits one older row at a time.
Each step must pass every rule below:

1. **Continuity.** No more than 20 sessions without a row between the two rows.
2. **The exchange's own link.** `PREVCLOSE(later) == CLOSE(earlier)` exactly. If they differ, that
   is an *adjustment break* (rule 4 decides it).
3. **No rename in between** (NSE `symbolchange.csv`, latest L0 snapshot).
   - A rename *into* the symbol means the walk moves to the old symbol and tests the link across
     the rename.
   - A rename *out of* the symbol, to anything else, means the symbol was vacated, and the walk
     stops.
4. **The ISIN existed then.** The serial digits of an `INE…01xx` ISIN count its re-issues. Every
   `isin_lineage` edge in the store goes from a lower serial to a higher one.
   - **Serial `01` (first issue):** this ISIN was never re-issued. A downward break with a gap of
     at most 5 sessions is admitted, and rule 5 does not apply.
   - **Serial ≥ 02 (re-issued):** the walk stops at any stored split or face-value change. It also
     stops at any break or discontinuity, unless a stored bonus, rights issue or dividend falls on
     that ex-date. On top of that, a **5-session settlement margin** after the boundary is
     refused. Measured on the ISIN era, NSE kept printing the old ISIN for 1–2 sessions after a
     split (TITAN 2011-06-23, CRISIL 2011-09-28/29).
5. **An ordinary session.** `CLOSE/PREVCLOSE` must stay within [0.7, 1.45]. The exchange did not
   always adjust PREVCLOSE on an ex-date. GRUH on 2012-07-24 shows PREVCLOSE 754.75 and CLOSE
   157.10, and carries a new ISIN from the next session.

Two more refusals:

- A row reached by two chains naming different ISINs is admitted by neither.
- Only `EQ`/`BE`/`BZ` rows form chains. `BL`/`IL`/`BT` rows take the ISIN of the same session's
  admitted chain row; in the ISIN era, 1,012/1,012 IL rows and 507/507 BL rows carry the EQ ISIN.
  All other series are out of scope, because they carry their own ISINs.

Every row that is not admitted is quarantined as `isin_column_absent:<reason>`.

**Validation against the exchange's published answer.** `pre_isin_promote validate` runs the same
resolver on the ISIN era with the ISINs hidden. Chains are anchored at each cutoff and walk back to
2011-06-22. Every admitted row is then compared with the ISIN NSE actually printed:

| anchor cutoff | rows judged | admitted | **wrong** |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 2013-01-01 | 589,353 | 551,459 | **0** |
| 2014-07-01 | 1,149,060 | 995,306 | **0** |
| 2016-01-01 | 1,735,233 | 1,422,131 | **0** |

The holdout also showed which rules matter. Without rule 5 the same runs admitted 707 / 3,239 /
7,560 wrong rows. Without the settlement margin they admitted 39 / 60 / 111. The stricter option
`--require-reissue-witness` (only admit a re-issued ISIN after a stored split dates the re-issue)
also gave 0 wrong, at about a third less coverage. It is off by default because, with rules 4–5 in
place, it removed nothing wrong in any holdout.

**Second witness: names in the PR bundle, 2010-01 → 2011-06.** For 36 sessions (two per month),
the PR bundle's `Pd` file `SECURITY` name was compared with `security_master.name` for the ISIN the
resolver chose. That is a different publication from the one the resolver reads.

- 37,674 rows were compared. 33,027 match on name tokens.
- The 151 distinct (symbol, ISIN) pairs that do not match were all reviewed by hand. Every one is a
  later company rename or an abbreviation:
  - ACKRUTI → Hubtown, CROMPGREAV → CG Power, SESAGOA → Vedanta, AKZOINDIA → JSW Dulux
  - KIRLOSIND, where "Kirloskar Oil Engines" is the pre-2010 name of today's Kirloskar Industries
  - and the rest are similar
- **0 contradictions found.**

**Third check: the 2011-06-21/22 boundary.** 1,493 of 1,506 anchored chains linked to their
2011-06-21 row with an exact PREVCLOSE equality. The other 13 admitted nothing.

**Tests that fail if the logic is inverted:** `tests/unit/test_pre_isin_resolver.py` and
`tests/unit/test_pre_isin_promote.py`. They cover:

- a reused symbol across a gap, and with no gap but a different PREVCLOSE;
- a split on a re-issued ISIN, including the settlement margin;
- a bonus on a first-issue ISIN;
- a break corroborated by a stored action vs. one contradicted by one;
- renames followed and never inferred;
- a vacated symbol;
- a doubly-claimed row;
- the real frozen 2011-06-21/22 fixture pair;
- row conservation and byte-determinism of the writer.
