## The bar: was 25 % reached, and on what

- **selection** (2016-09-02 → 2021-08-31): no arm cleared 25 % — best 14.68% (M10.7 + regime gate).
- **verification** (2021-09-01 → 2026-08-31): no arm cleared 25 % — best 18.55% (Swing composite (M10.7)).

**Answer: not on the windows this run measured** (selection, verification). That is a statement about these windows only: a window this run did not sweep cannot be reported on here, and `ops/gates/M12-strategy-sweep-sixyear.md` is the case in point — the M10.7 composite clears 25 % on 2019-07..2026-08 at the ₹1 crore floor. A verdict that silently generalised from the windows it happened to hold would be making exactly the claim this task exists to prevent.

## Rank stability across the split

Spearman rank correlation between the selection and verification orderings is **0.372** — weak. Three of the five arms chosen as best stayed in the top five; `Mean proximity (50d)` went from 20th to 3rd and `Short composite + regime gate` from 9th to 21st. Read individual ranks as noisy.

What *is* stable is the family. The M10.7 composite and its variants take ranks 1, 2, 3 and 6 on the selection window and 4, 1, 2 and 6 on the verification window — the only group that holds the top of both tables. Every short-composite arm sits in the bottom half of both. The evidence supports "this family beats that family", not "this arm is the best arm".

## What this verdict cannot be asked to prove

- **A walk-forward with one split is one draw.** It says the selected arm did or did not hold up across a single boundary, not that it holds up across boundaries in general.
- **Both windows share a lake, a cost model and a fill model.** An error common to both — the 10 bp base slippage at the low liquidity floor, the price-return benchmark proxy — moves selection and verification together and is invisible to this comparison.
- **Survivorship in the universe is handled; survivorship in the *signals* is not.** The legs swept here are the ones this repo built because earlier work suggested they worked, which is a selection effect no split inside this lake can undo.
