## What was run

D13 (`PAPER_RATIFIED_2026_09_06`) reads the regime — NIFTY 50 TRI against its 200-session average — only on the monthly rebalance session. The variants (M14.5 switches on D13's own parameters, nothing else changed):

* **Momentum v2, D13 paper config** — the paper book's configuration (D13): M9.5's toggles without the 15% vol target.
* **D13 + daily regime re-entry** — monthly exit; a parked book re-enters the first session the index is >= its 200d MA.
* **D13 + daily regime re-entry and exit** — the regime read every session, both ways: park on risk-off, re-enter on risk-on.
* **D13 + daily regime re-entry, 2% band** — daily re-entry only once the index is >= 1.02 x its 200d MA (whipsaw damping).

Mandate (owner, 2026-09-07): rank on XIRR ÷ max drawdown; both liquidity floors (₹1 cr and ₹10 cr median daily turnover); the decade, the six-year window and the walk-forward (choose on 2016-09..2021-08, verify on 2021-09..2026-08), never averaged; bar: backtested XIRR > 25%. Book corporate actions and interest on idle cash on; every order through A8's ratified rails.

Columns: **Parks / re-entries** are regime switches — the policy's own triggers replayed over the published TRI and calendar (`backtest.m14_5_report.switches`); in brackets, how many the fill ledger confirms (a sale, resp. a purchase, on the following session). The confirmation is weak for a switch made on a monthly rebalance, where the ordinary rebalance trades anyway; it is informative only for the mid-month (daily) switches. Parks count entries into the parked state; while parked, the monthly rule re-issues the park every risk-off rebalance (counted in the ceiling below). **One-way turnover** is (buys + sells) ÷ 2 ÷ mean NAV ÷ years. **Charges** are every brokerage, STT, stamp, exchange and GST rupee the shared cost model charged (₹10L opening book). **A8 min-holdings refusals** are *all* sells A8's 8-name floor refused over the run, on any session (the run summary's rail blocks). In brackets, the sessions on which a park was issued. One park refuses at most as many sells as the floor itself — 8 under the ratified rails, since the rail bites only on a sell taking a book at the floor below it (`analyst/rails/engine.py`) — so refusals beyond 8 per park session happened on sessions that were not parks, such as a re-entry rebalance trying to sell a kept name that has left the band.

## Analysis and recommendation (hand-written, 2026-10-08; corrected after review)

*Everything in this section is written by hand from the tables above. It is kept verbatim when
the tables are re-rendered. Counts of cells won, lost and tied come from the generated scorecard
above, not from hand tallies; other figures are copied from the tables.*

### The baseline is the baseline

D13 here is a fresh replay, not a copy. It reproduces the saved M12.R campaign
(`~/campaign/m12-rerun-2bb2b08`) exactly in all eight floor-only cells: for example decade ₹1 cr
25.06% / 28.71%, ₹10 cr 24.14% / 30.05%, and verification ₹1 cr 33.04% / 17.10%. The code check
is in the tests: D13's parameter `repr` (its run fingerprint) is unchanged by M14.5, and a replay
with the switches off reproduces, byte-for-byte, the journal, book and rails digest of the policy
before M14.5.

### Reading the scorecard

* **Daily re-entry, the variant the index proxy favoured, is rejected.** Its XIRR/DD is worse
  than D13's in all 12 cells, and its max drawdown is deeper in all 12 (by up to 11.22pp).
  The proxy that motivated it comes from the read-only `explore-regime-reentry-cadence`
  investigation that scoped this task, not from this campaign. That proxy held the NIFTY 50 TRI
  itself, and found monthly exit with daily re-entry at 9.47% XIRR / 15.02% DD against 8.71% /
  19.52% for the monthly rule. The same investigation counted 77 crossings of the 200-session
  average since 2016, 61% of them reversing within 5 sessions. This task's own read of the
  published TRI over 2016-09-01..2026-09-10 reproduces the 77 crossings; the 61% figure is the
  investigation's.
  The proxy does not carry over to the stock book. A re-entry on the first session the index
  regains its average buys a fresh top-20 of high-beta momentum names at crossings that mostly
  reverse. Because exits stay monthly, a failed re-entry is then held for up to a month.
* **Daily re-entry and exit is rejected.** Its XIRR/DD is worse in 11 of 12 cells. Its max
  drawdown is deeper in 11 of 12; the exception is NIFTY 500 verification at ₹10 cr, 0.14pp
  shallower. It parks about four times as often (39 parks over the decade against D13's 9).
  One-way turnover is 3.16–3.71× a year against D13's 1.99–2.65×. Charges are 42% higher on the
  ₹1 cr decade (₹3.13L vs ₹2.20L) and 25% higher on the ₹10 cr decade (₹2.22L vs ₹1.78L).
  Exiting on the first breakdown does not cap the drawdown: on the ₹10 cr decade it is 42.03%
  against 30.05%.
* **The 2% banded re-entry wins more cells than it loses, but it loses the cells the mandate
  decides on.**
  * Its XIRR/DD beats D13's in 9 of 12 cells. Those are every ₹10 cr cell (the ₹10 cr selection
    window by 0.67 to 0.65) and all four NIFTY 500 cells. NIFTY 500 at ₹1 cr is the paper book's
    own universe (`UniverseParameters()` = `nifty500`, ₹1 cr floor): six-year 28.39% / 20.82%
    against 26.65% / 21.57%, and verification 26.97% / 19.85% against 22.79% / 22.03%.
  * It loses exactly the three ₹1 cr floor-only cells: decade, walk-forward selection and
    walk-forward verification.
  * Its max drawdown is deeper in 4 of 12 cells, an exact tie in 1 (verification ₹1 cr, 17.10%
    for both) and shallower in 7.

### A8's 8-name floor limits every regime rule (known; not changed here)

* **What the rail does.** A8's minimum-holdings rail refuses any sell that would take a book
  holding 8 or more names below 8. A risk-off park therefore sells the basket down to 8 names,
  and the rest stays invested through the risk-off spell. Every arm, D13 included, keeps at least
  8 names while parked.
* **This was raised before.** `ops/gates/ai-analyst-plan-2026-09-07.md`: "`MIN_HOLDINGS=8` makes
  the regime gate structurally unimplementable".
* **The refusals column counts every refusal, not just park refusals.** It counts every sell the
  rail refused on any session. A park can refuse at most 8 sells (`analyst/rails/engine.py`:
  the rail bites only on the sell that takes a book at the floor below it). The bracketed figure
  is the number of sessions a park was issued, so parks can explain at most 8 × that figure.
* **Parks explain at most about 80% of D13's refusals.** D13 has 24 risk-off rebalances in the
  ₹1 cr decade, so parks can account for at most 192 refusals, against 241 recorded. On
  selection it is at most 72 of 91, and on verification at most 120 of 153.
* **The rest happen on non-park sessions.** The likely case is a re-entry rebalance trying to
  sell a kept name that has since left the band; that has not been traced trade by trade. So the
  floor does more than leave a parked book partly invested: it also slows the clean-up after a
  re-entry.
* **Why the proxy over-states.** This is also why an index-only proxy, which goes fully to cash,
  over-states what any regime-timing rule can do for this book.
* **This is an owner decision.** How a regime park should relate to the floor (exempt a park
  from the rail, or accept a partial park) is a decision about the ratified rails. M14.5 does not
  touch it.
