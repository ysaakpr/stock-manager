## What was run

Pre-registered in `ops/studies/preregistration-m14-10-nifty500-walk-forward-2026-11-06.md` before any run. The universe is the PIT NIFTY 500 screen (the paper book's own), whose membership history covers from 2015-03-28 (M14.1 build of 2026-10-10). Each arm is M14.9's exactly, plus one pre-specified combination:

* **Momentum v2, D13 paper config** — the paper book's configuration (D13): M9.5's toggles without the 15% vol target.
* **V1 confirmed cross (5 sessions >= MA200)** — a parked book re-enters after the NIFTY 50 TRI has closed at or above its 200d MA on 5 consecutive sessions; exits monthly, as D13.
* **V2 momentum turn (MA50 rising, 20d return > 0, >= 0.98 MA200)** — a parked book re-enters when the TRI is >= 0.98 x its 200d MA, its 50d MA is above its value 10 sessions earlier and its 20-session return is positive; exits monthly.
* **S+refill: capped names replaced by the next-ranked outside the sector** — as S, and a basket name the cap would refuse is replaced by the next-ranked name outside the capped sector, up to 20 names.
* **V2 + S+refill (momentum turn with the sector refill)** — V2's momentum-turn re-entry and S+refill's rank-ordered, sector-refilled buys together; every rebalance, the early re-entry included, uses the refill.

Mandate (owner, 2026-09-07): rank on XIRR ÷ max drawdown; both liquidity floors (₹1 cr and ₹10 cr median daily turnover); the decade, the six-year window and the walk-forward (choose on 2016-09..2021-08, verify on 2021-09..2026-08), never averaged; bar: backtested XIRR > 25%. Book corporate actions and interest on idle cash on; every order through A8's ratified rails.
