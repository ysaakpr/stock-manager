Backup produces a `pg_dump` + checksummed manifest (`SHA256SUMS`, `backup.json`, `row_counts.tsv`,

sub-boxes met.

not copied*, and nothing is uploaded off-host. §8.1's object-storage backup is deferred (a bucket is a
spending decision, AGENTIC_CONTEXT §3.9). Today the manifest buys *detection* of L0 corruption, not
off-host recovery. Recorded here so the gate is not read as green on off-host durability.
