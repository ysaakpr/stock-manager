# Cap-tier strategies: measurement and verdict (X2, 2026-10-05)

Owner request (2026-10-05): *run a strategy that holds the best-performing small caps and compare drawdown with
return; build multi-cap, focused-midcap and focused-smallcap strategies and measure them against the current
strategies over all the data we have.*

Branch `feat/cap-tier-strategies`. Runs were made at `ccd61f1` from the primary lake
(`/home/ubuntu/stock-manager/data`, asserted before the run) into `~/campaign/cap-tiers-ccd61f1/`:
56 runs, 0 failed. Reproduce with `uv run python -m backtest.cap_tier_campaign run|render --out DIR
--data-root /home/ubuntu/stock-manager/data --workers 2`.

## What was measured

- **Size measure, chosen before any strategy existed** (`ops/studies/cap-tier-size-measure-2026-10-05.md`):
  PIT market cap is not available from 2013 to 2017 (0 % of the liquid universe has a dated share count). The
  tiers therefore rank the trailing 126-session median of close x quantity among NSE EQ equities:
  1-100 / 101-250 / 251-500. They are **liquidity-rank tiers that proxy AMFI cap tiers**. Against real market cap
  (`mcap`, 2024-02..2026-08) the Spearman correlation is 0.916. The proxy puts 68.2 % of real large caps, 48.3 %
  of real mid caps and 45.1 % of real small caps in the same tier as AMFI would.
- **Arms**, fixed before any return was seen (`backtest/sweep.py` `CAP_TIER_ARMS`). The signal, cadence,
  trailing stop, re-underwrite and cash interest are all M10.7's, unchanged. Each sleeve's sell band is 3x its
  basket, as in M10.7. No new parameter was added and none was tuned.
  - Multi cap: top 8 of each of LARGE, MID and SMALL by within-tier composite rank.
  - Focused midcap: top 20 of MID.
  - Focused smallcap: top 20 of SMALL.
  - The composite is struck over the whole liquid candidate set, then ranked within each tier. A tier with too
    few names leaves its slots empty.
- **Path.** All arms run through the same RailGate, SimBroker, shared cost model and tax path. Rails are
  untouched (`analyst/rails/` has no diff).
- **Comparison, same runs and settings.** Swing composite (M10.7), M10.7 + regime gate, Momentum v2 and Naive
  momentum, plus three published TRIs: NIFTY 50, NIFTY Midcap 150 and NIFTY Smallcap 250. Midcap 150 and
  Smallcap 250 were fetched 2026-10-05 in 2 requests. Their earliest level is 2005-04-01 (base 1000). Both
  launched 2016-04-01, so levels before that are NSE's back-computed series.

## Verdict

**No cap-tier arm improves on the swing composite's return per drawdown at the ₹10 crore floor over the full
2012-07-04 → 2026-08-31 window.** Full window, ₹10 crore floor:

| strategy | XIRR pre-tax | after-tax (realised) | max DD | return/DD |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Multi cap | 22.15 % | 18.78 % | 25.41 % | 0.87 |
| Focused midcap | 20.09 % | 17.56 % | 25.65 % | 0.78 |
| Focused smallcap | 12.12 % | 10.18 % | 57.79 % | 0.21 |
| Swing composite (M10.7) | 20.43 % | 17.41 % | 23.01 % | 0.89 |
| M10.7 + regime gate | 18.77 % | 15.77 % | 18.06 % | 1.04 |
| NIFTY 50 / Midcap 150 / Smallcap 250 TRI | 12.63 / 19.09 / 16.71 % | — | 38.27 / 43.06 / 59.78 % | 0.33 / 0.44 / 0.28 |

- **Multi cap** earns about 1.7 pp more than M10.7 pre-tax (+1.4 pp after tax) for about 2.4 pp more drawdown.
  It is not robust across folds: at ₹10 crore its after-tax XIRR is 2.55 % in F1 against M10.7's 9.75 %, then
  36.74 % against 36.61 % in F2, and 17.78 % against 16.91 % in F3.
- **Focused smallcap at ₹10 crore** measures the floor, not the strategy. Until about 2021 almost no name ranked
  251-500 has a 365-day median turnover of ₹10 crore or more (the rank-500 name traded ₹0.3-3 crore/day; see the
  study). For most of the history the arm had few or no candidates. It held a handful of names and took the
  full 2018-20 small-cap crash: -52.3 % against the Smallcap 250 TRI's -54.6 %.
- **Focused smallcap at ₹1 crore** earns 24.66 % pre-tax with a 26.89 % max drawdown (ratio 0.92), against
  M10.7's 24.83 % / 23.76 % at the same floor. In the 2018-20 crash it lost 16.3 % against the Smallcap 250
  TRI's 54.6 %. This floor's cost model is unvalidated for small-cap impact cost (below).
- Every arm beats its size benchmark on return per drawdown on the full window, and so does every existing
  strategy. Measured against the TRIs, the tier arms add nothing that M10.7 does not already have.
- **Concentration of luck, smallcap arm, full window.** The top 5 names made 14.7 % of profit at ₹1 crore and
  20.7 % at ₹10 crore. For comparison, M10.7 is at 13.5 % / 13.6 % and Naive momentum at 30.2 % / 28.3 %.

## Caveats (stated as required)

1. **Trial count.** These are 3 new trials on data that has already been looked at. The trial count for any
   deflated-Sharpe correction becomes **≥ 31**: the pre-registration's ≥ 28, plus these 3. No DSR or decision
   rule was evaluated here, and nothing above is a pre-registered test.
2. **The ₹1 crore floor's cost model is unvalidated for small-cap impact cost.** Every ₹1 crore number carries
   this, and the report labels each ₹1 crore table with it. At that floor the median basket name trades only a
   few crore a day, and the fill model's slippage there is a claim, not a measurement.
3. **Survivorship and names that stopped printing.** The raw lake keeps delisted names, so they are in the
   universe while they trade. A holding that stops printing cannot be sold, because there is no bar to fill
   against. It is valued at its **last printed close until the end**: never written down, and never credited a
   merger consideration (the store's mergers name no surviving ISIN). Focused smallcap, full window:
   - ₹1 crore: 7 names stopped printing while held, worth ₹17.58 lakh = 7.74 % of final NAV. Two have a MERGER
     row in the store.
   - ₹10 crore: 3 names, worth ₹3.89 lakh = 7.69 % of final NAV, one with a MERGER row.

   Every arm's count is in the 'stuck at end' column, and every name is listed below.
4. **The proxy is a liquidity tier.** About half of the "smallcap" arm's names are AMFI small caps. Most of the
   rest are real micro caps that trade like small caps (study §2).
5. **Sector cap.** `MAX_SECTOR` is the dominant rail block for every arm, the existing ones included. Sector
   data is a current snapshot only (pre-registration §5), so the cap mostly binds on the unknown-sector bucket.
   That split by bucket is not measured here: the rail-block counts are by rail only. No rail was loosened.
6. **End cash** is high for several arms, M10.7 included (55 % at ₹1 crore on the full window). *Corrected
   2026-10-05 (`fix/cap-tier-cash-report`); the original text is kept, struck through, below.* The cause was
   measured from the saved ledgers and NAVs, with nothing replayed (`backtest.idle_cash`; re-rendered report
   `~/campaign/cap-tiers-ccd61f1-cash-rerender/reports/cap-tiers.md`). Each NAV session's cash is split three
   ways. **Waiting proceeds**: sale cash since the last buy session. A buy is sized from cash that is already
   free, never from its own session's sales, so those proceeds sit until the next rebalance. **Ceiling
   leftover**: the rest, after a buy session in which at least one buy hit min(₹1.2 lakh, 15 % of NAV).
   **Other leftover**: everything else. Means over NAV sessions, as a share of NAV:

   | window, floor | strategy | mean cash | waiting proceeds | ceiling leftover | other | buys at ceiling |
   |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
   | full, ₹1 cr | Multi cap | 21.65 % | 13.21 % | 5.67 % | 2.78 % | 1476 of 3889 |
   | full, ₹1 cr | Focused midcap | 17.74 % | 10.22 % | 4.24 % | 3.28 % | 1111 of 2756 |
   | full, ₹1 cr | Focused smallcap | 26.57 % | 11.76 % | 9.33 % | 5.48 % | 1631 of 3371 |
   | full, ₹1 cr | Swing composite (M10.7) | 31.49 % | 14.35 % | 13.49 % | 3.65 % | 1874 of 3924 |
   | full, ₹10 cr | Multi cap | 17.46 % | 12.12 % | 3.80 % | 1.54 % | 1232 of 3170 |
   | full, ₹10 cr | Focused midcap | 14.15 % | 9.97 % | 2.64 % | 1.55 % | 852 of 2420 |
   | full, ₹10 cr | Focused smallcap | 46.10 % | 8.38 % | 4.37 % | 33.34 % | 207 of 1285 |
   | full, ₹10 cr | Swing composite (M10.7) | 18.01 % | 11.41 % | 4.77 % | 1.84 % | 1141 of 2857 |

   - **The primary cause is waiting proceeds.** For the three tier arms and M10.7 they run 9.4-18.4 % of
     NAV in every window and at both floors. The one exception is focused smallcap at ₹10 crore on the full
     window, at 8.4 %. The regime-gated M10.7 holds more (14.7-28.4 %), because its gate holds sale cash back
     on purpose. In the fold test windows, waiting proceeds are 46-97 % of all cash for every arm except two:
     Momentum v2, whose cash is mostly "other", and focused smallcap at ₹10 crore in F1.
   - **The per-order ceiling is the secondary cause.** It binds an equal-weight 20-name buy only once the book
     passes about ₹24 lakh. The fold test windows start at ₹10 lakh, and there ceiling leftover is 0.0-0.4 % of
     NAV, with three exceptions: focused smallcap at ₹10 crore in F1 (12.5 %) and F2 (7.2 %), and Momentum v2
     in F3 (4.4 % / 1.7 %). On the full window the book compounds to crores, and ceiling leftover reaches
     2.6-14.1 % of NAV. Only for M10.7 at ₹1 crore does it come close to the waiting proceeds (13.5 % against
     14.4 %). **Scaling that ₹1.2 lakh ceiling is still an open owner decision** (pre-registration §5). This
     report does not change it.
   - **Focused smallcap at ₹10 crore had an empty tier.** It made no buy on 111 consecutive decision sessions,
     from 2012-07-04 to its first buy on 2017-01-02, and held 100 % cash over that span. The audit traced this to the floor: no
     name ranked 251-500 cleared ₹10 crore. That accounts for its 33.34 % "other" cash. Its headline full-window
     XIRR (12.12 % pre-tax) includes those 4½ years of cash. Measured from 2016-12-30, the decision session
     before its first buy, its XIRR is 14.58 %. That second figure is **informational only** and does not
     replace the headline. The report now flags any arm with more than 25 buy-free decision sessions (one
     trading year at the swing cadence; the longest regime-gate stand-aside in these runs was 17). This is the
     only arm flagged.
   - So the idle cash does **not** drag every arm alike. Waiting proceeds weigh on every arm. The ceiling weighs
     mostly on full-window runs, and most of all on M10.7 at ₹1 crore. The empty tier weighs only on focused smallcap
     at ₹10 crore.

   ~~This is the ₹1.2 lakh per-order cap against a book grown to crores. That cap still awaits an owner decision
   on scaling (pre-registration §5). It drags every arm alike.~~ *Superseded: an attribution guess, never
   measured. The measurement above shows the ceiling is the secondary cause, waiting proceeds the primary, and
   the drag is not alike across arms.*
7. **Trades** counts fills (buys and sells), not round trips.
