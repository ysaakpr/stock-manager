What the numbers say:

- **Daily band exit** turns over the book much more: 2,250 buys against D13's 1,109, and charges
  ×1.7. It wins F1 and F2 but loses F3 by 4.9 pp. It deepens the drawdown in every window: a name
  sold mid-month is replaced by topping up the rest of the basket, which concentrates the book
  (MAX_SECTOR blocks rise from 84 to 452). Over the continuous decade it gives up 3.3 pp of
  after-tax XIRR and adds 5.8 pp of drawdown.
- **Daily drift trim 25%** barely moves the book: 141 trims in ten years. It cut F3's drawdown by
  6.1 pp, but that is one window, and it lost F1 and F2. Its mean is 0.21 pp below D13, inside the
  noise the DSR measures.
- **At ₹1 crore/day (information only, decides nothing)** both arms look a little better than D13
  over the continuous decade. After-tax XIRR: band exit 21.70%, drift trim 21.86%, D13 21.35%. Max
  DD: 26.94%, 25.84% and 28.71%. Fold by fold the band exit is again mixed: F3 is 7.1 pp worse and
  its drawdown is 18.8 pp deeper. The mandate puts the edge in the thinner half of the universe.
  This is consistent with that, and it is not a result: it was not the pre-registered test, and
  any follow-up is a new trial.
- **The exit count is decisions, not fills.** It includes sells the rails refused and the policy
  offered again the next day. At ₹1 cr the band exit's F1 run decided 710 exits and filled 196
  sells; the MIN_HOLDINGS 8-name floor refused the rest. The rails were never bypassed.

## Bug found during the run, fixed, rerun (pre-registration §7)

The first arms pass (commit `a0be143`, launched 12:33 IST) stopped at 13:16 IST. The drift-trim arm's
continuous run hit `DuplicateStagedOrderError`. On 2026-01-05 the next-session redeploy bought
INE872H01027 while the drift trim sold it, and SimBroker refuses a second staged order for one ISIN.
The cause: the shared allocator (`simulate_sip_instalment`) sizes buys against *settled* holdings and
free cash, while the trim values the whole book, shares in settlement included. So the redeploy saw
the name as underweight and the trim saw it as overweight.

The pre-registration already says a name being sold that session is never bought (§3). Commit
`d255341` enforces this on the redeploy and on the rebalance session. It drops the buy, keeps the
budget in cash, and journals a `buys_dropped_sold_today` evidence item. Two regression tests fail
with the fix reverted. The arm's definition did not change.

Runs that had already finished were reused. Each of them completed without a collision (the broker
would have refused one), so the fix cannot change them. The baseline does not reach this code, and
its frozen record (`frozen-baseline.json`, struck at `a0be143`) re-verified on `d255341`, every
digest and file hash unchanged. The arms phase was resumed at 13:39 IST and finished at 14:09 IST;
the render finished at 14:19 IST.
